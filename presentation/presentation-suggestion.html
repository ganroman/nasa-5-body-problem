Q&A / 1:30–2:00 / 30 seconds

One question. One clear answer.

Suggested presentation

Let the jury choose the question. Answer in roughly 15–20 seconds. Use the remaining time for the question and handoff.

Ready answers

Does it certify safety?
“It supports evidence review and identifies missing validation. Approval stays with the responsible authority.”

What does AI do?
“The planned AI explains sourced findings and computed differences. The current mock has no live AI connection.”

What works today?
“The material interaction and scenario export work with dummy data. NASA source files are present; verified dashboard integration remains to be completed.”

If nobody asks: briefly explain that evidence for a casing does not establish safety of the whole instrument.

Delivery checklist

Make the two minutes feel effortless.

  • One speaker, one teammate controlling slides and playback.
  • Rehearse to 1:30 before questions; transitions count.
  • Use the same cargo and component throughout.
  • Embed media locally and check sound and accurate captions.
  • Keep a product screenshot ready if playback fails.
  • Remove risk scores and unverified prediction claims from the video.
Evidence & scope

Keep claims matched to the build.

This plan follows the cargo build plan, the video review, and the official judging criteria: Impact, Creativity, Validity, Relevance and Presentation.

The repository prototype explicitly uses dummy data. Neither these slide mockups nor the original video establish validated fire prediction, cargo approval, or an integrated AI feature.

The two-minute local pitch is separate from the global submission demo format: a video up to 30 seconds or a deck up to seven slides.
