Cargo Passport | Five-Body Problem | Flame in Freefall | NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, Vienna

Team: Roman Slysh, Roman Ganopolskyi, Ivan Kotelnikov, Gjergj Brestovci, Sophie Ganopolska

Every shipment to space carries a Cargo Passport: each item and the materials it is made of. Follow the cargo from Earth to the ISS and the Moon, compare the conditions at every stop, and see which NASA PSI combustion tests apply, what differs and what evidence is missing. Next: predict flame spread in microgravity, check it against real PSI runs, and extend to the Moon and Mars.

Data shown: material and experiment names come from the NASA PSI tables (PMMA in BASS-II at 14–22% O2, SIBAL in Saffire-I and silicone in Saffire-II at about 21% O2; none tested at the lunar 30% O2, 0.69 atm or 1/6 g). The shipment, its items and the flame animation are illustrative. The video compares evidence; it is not a safety verdict. Lunar habitat conditions (30% O2, 0.69 atm) come from the challenge brief.
