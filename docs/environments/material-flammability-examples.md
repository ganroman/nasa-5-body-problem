# Material flammability evidence: representative examples per environment

Research reference for the **Flame in Freefall** challenge · Team Five-Body Problem · 14 November 2026

This document applies the sources in section 3 of the [data sources catalogue](../../data/data-sources-catalog.md#3-material-flammability-and-model-properties) (material flammability and model properties) to one representative cargo item per environment. Each example shows what the evidence establishes, and what it leaves unresolved, as a [cargo passport](../cargo-passport.md) would present it.

The examples are illustrative research cases, not safety findings, qualification decisions or confirmed mission hardware.

## Why material evidence matters here

The PSI solid-material tests (BASS, BASS-II, Saffire) sit at or just below 21% O₂. The challenge's lunar-habitat scenario is **≥30% O₂ at ≤10.2 psi**, so PSI alone cannot cover habitat materials at elevated oxygen. See the [research brief](../research-brief.md#a-fact-from-the-tables-oxygen-coverage). Section 3 sources can partly fill that gap with material-specific ground tests.

## 1. NASA lunar surface habitat

Environment reference: [NASA lunar surface base](lunar-base-nasa.md).

**Item:** webbing used in a restraint net or storage-bag handle, unpacked and used inside the habitat. Softgoods like this accompany almost any cargo.

**Intended environment:** the challenge scenario of ≥30% O₂ at ≤10.2 psi, in approximately one-sixth Earth gravity. This is a scenario, not a confirmed NASA cabin specification.

| Section 3 source | Role for this item |
|---|---|
| [NASA TB 26-05: Webbings for Use in Elevated Oxygen Environments](https://www.nasa.gov/centers-and-facilities/nesc/tb-26-05-webbings-for-use-in-elevated-oxygen-environments/) (31 July 2026; revised PDF dated September 2026) | Two one-inch Sturges Manufacturing webbings, both 60% Kevlar® / 40% polybenzimidazole (PBI). **Natural (gold): maximum oxygen concentration (MOC) 37% O₂ at 8.2 psia, passing NASA-STD-6001B Test 1. Black: MOC 35% O₂ at 8.2 psia.** MOC is the highest oxygen concentration at which at least five samples pass at a fixed pressure. The bulletin names crew mobility aids, restraint nets and storage-bag handles as applications for future lunar and Martian missions. |
| [NASA-STD-6001](https://standards.nasa.gov/standard/NASA/NASA-STD-6001), revision B, Test 1 | The upward flame-propagation method behind the result. Tested in an unshielded J configuration, with the igniter impinging on the front surface including the lateral free edge. Defines what "pass" means. |
| [NASA White Sands Test Facility](https://www.nasa.gov/centers-and-facilities/white-sands/flammability/) | Test facility. Reports: WSTF 26-49149 (natural, flammability and offgassing) and WSTF 26-49145 (black, flammability). |
| [MAPTIS](https://maptis.nasa.gov/) | Detailed records 73441 (natural) and 73442 (black). Access and reuse not verified. |
| [NIST Material Flammability Characterization](https://www.nist.gov/programs-projects/material-flammability-characterization) | Not used for this example. Relevant only if a modelling extension is added. |

**Observed evidence:** both webbings passed above 30% O₂ at a pressure inside the scenario band. The natural and black variants differ by two percentage points of oxygen (37% vs 35%). The same fibre blend in a different colour gave a different result, so the passport must match the exact product and colour, not a generic label such as "Kevlar webbing".

**Gaps the passport must show:**

- **Gravity:** ground tests in Earth gravity. Behaviour in lunar gravity is not established, and PSI contains no partial-gravity combustion data.
- **Pressure:** tested only at 8.2 psia. Other habitat pressures are untested.
- **Wear and assembly:** the bulletin states that wear was not assessed and that textile results do not guarantee finished-assembly performance. Stitching, hook-and-loop, edge finish, weave and adjacent materials remain unknown.
- **PSI link:** no PSI investigation tested these webbings.

**Illustrative dashboard wording:**

> The exact webbing (natural Kevlar/PBI) passed NASA-STD-6001B Test 1 up to 37% O₂ at 8.2 psia in Earth-gravity ground tests. Applicability under lunar gravity, in the finished bag, or after wear remains unresolved.
