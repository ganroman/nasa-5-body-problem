The whole vehicle

Concept: not built, written as what would happen.

Suppose every item has a passport and a tag that says where it is. Then the whole vehicle could be seen at once.

Picking an item would light it up with its checks. The same rules would run for every item each time the place or the air changes.

Flag raised by the place (Charging shelf); Enclosure: conditions differ; Battery pack: no evidence; Wiring: no evidence; Carry strap: conditions differ

  • Enclosure PMMA cast sheet Conditions differ

    In the Saffire IV and V tests, both concurrent PMMA samples (IV-2 and V-3) did not spread appreciably and instead remained fixed at the leading edge of the sample, advancing through surface regression only, which the authors call typical for thick fuels in low gravity but different from normal gravity.5

  • Battery pack Lithium-ion cells No evidence

    Related records, not a test of this material

    The JSC 29353D handbook says small, low-voltage alkaline and coin batteries are still believed to be incapable of delivering the powers required for ignition, although larger batteries such as lithium-ion rechargeable batteries for portable electronic devices can certainly achieve higher power levels.6

    Stowage bags can have flammable items stowed inside provided they do not contain ignition sources (such as electrical power) and are not susceptible to spontaneous ignition or chemical reaction, and the ISS and MPCV Programs allow fully certified and undamaged batteries, including lithium-ion rechargeable batteries, to be stowed inside such bags without additional containment.7

  • Gaskets Silicone rubber, ~1.5 mm Evidence applies

    In a table of oxygen-concentration flammability limits at three total pressures, silicone is listed at 28% oxygen at 48.2 kPa, 23% at 85.3 kPa and 21% at 101.3 kPa.8

  • Wiring PTFE-insulated wire No evidence

    Related records, not a test of this material

    In a table of oxygen-concentration flammability limits at three total pressures, PTFE is listed with a maximum oxygen concentration (MOC) of 53% oxygen at 48.2 kPa, 46% at 85.3 kPa and 42% at 101.3 kPa.9

    For flammability considerations, any high-power electrical wire is a potential ignition source, and any item that could cause sparks, such as a brush motor, is also considered a potential ignition source.10

  • Carry strap Kevlar/PBI webbing, black Conditions differ

    The webbings had maximum oxygen concentrations (MOCs) of 37% for the natural webbing and 35% for the black webbing at 8.2 psia.11

About this place

The text of the ISS operational control OCAD 101882 says that, when deployed in the open cabin for use, the flammable items it covers will be kept away from rack power outlets, utility outlet panels (UOPs) and power strips.2

Same for every component here

Flammable materials stowed in these containers do not constitute a fire risk while in the containers provided they are unpowered, but powered payloads or experiments stowed in lockers for an entire mission, or located in place of a locker, are to be evaluated from a flammability standpoint because they present a potential fire hazard.1
